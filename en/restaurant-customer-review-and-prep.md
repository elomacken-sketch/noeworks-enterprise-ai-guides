# How can restaurants connect customer review analysis with daily prep and wastage control?

> **Direct Answer**: Customer complaints and kitchen wastage are closely linked: inaccurate prep leads to peak-hour stock-outs or over-held ingredients, which immediately trigger negative reviews. Instead of using AI merely to write polite reply templates, use it to map multi-platform reviews to specific dishes, shifts, and kitchen stations—and connect those insights to daily prep sheets and end-of-day wastage checks. This guide explains working methods, not a completed customer case.

## Map dine-in and delivery reviews to specific dishes, shifts, and stations

Tracking overall star ratings across review and delivery platforms rarely tells a kitchen what to fix tomorrow. AI categorises every review by store, service window (lunch rush, dinner, late-night delivery), menu item, and defect type (temperature, seasoning, portion size, ticket delay, or packaging spill) so store managers see exact station bottlenecks.

## Trigger internal kitchen verification before sending customer replies

When a review flags food quality, foreign objects, or missing items, the workflow first prompts the store manager to check batch prep logs and pack-line records. AI can draft a tailored response, but staff verify the facts and approve any refund or recovery offer before replying.

## Generate daily prep reference ranges from POS history and calendar signals

Relying solely on gut feel often causes high waste on rainy weekends or stock-outs during promotional campaigns. By combining four to eight weeks of day-of-week POS item sales with active promotions, weather forecasts, and local events, AI prepares a daily prep reference sheet for high-cost proteins and short-shelf-life prep items.

## Compare theoretical POS usage against actual end-of-day waste

At close, compare theoretical ingredient usage implied by POS sales against actual kitchen requisitions and waste logs. Persistent variances on key items alert the head chef and store manager to check portioning tools, thawing schedules, or storage temperatures.

## Common Questions

### Can we start if we only have POS exports and daily manual waste logs rather than a full ERP?

Yes. Rather than tracking every spice and garnish on day one, start with the top 10 to 15 high-cost proteins and short-shelf-life prep items using daily POS sales exports and closing count sheets.

### Should AI send purchase orders directly to food suppliers?

No. AI calculates recommended prep and order ranges, while the head chef and store manager verify current walk-in inventory, group bookings, and supplier quality before confirming orders.

### How do we prevent stores from hiding waste numbers to look good on paper?

Cross-check reported waste against customer review trends: if a store reports near-zero waste while complaints about stale texture or peak-hour 86'd items rise, the system flags the discrepancy for area manager audit.
