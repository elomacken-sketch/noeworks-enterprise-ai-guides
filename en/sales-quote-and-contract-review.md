# How can sales teams use AI to check quotation definitions and screen contract exceptions?

> **Direct Answer**: Late-stage deals often stall because a revised quote has mismatched line-item totals or a client paper contract introduces open-ended acceptance terms and extended payment windows. While AI never replaces legal counsel or management pricing authority, it can pre-screen quotations and contracts against your commercial playbook—flagging math discrepancies, discount thresholds, and non-standard risk clauses before approval routing. This guide explains working methods, not a completed customer case.

## Check quotation scope, unit definitions, and discount thresholds before sending

Under deadline pressure, sales reps frequently update a headline price without adjusting line-item hours, tax assumptions, or complimentary add-ons. AI checks draft quotes against standard rate cards and margin rules, verifying arithmetic, tax basis, and whether the proposed discount or bundle requires sales director or finance approval.

## Cross-check quotation schedules against contract body clauses

When pricing tables go through three rounds of negotiation, the main contract text often retains outdated milestone names or deliverable counts from version one. AI cross-checks the pricing annex, statement of work, and master contract to ensure total contract value, payment percentages, and milestone definitions match word for word.

## Screen client redlines against a five-point commercial risk checklist

When a client returns a redlined draft or insists on their own procurement template, AI screens the text against company commercial guardrails: payment terms and triggers, deemed-acceptance windows, liability caps, background IP retention, and termination conditions—producing a one-page exception summary.

## Pair flagged exceptions with pre-approved fallback language

Instead of bouncing a contract back to sales with a vague rejection, the exception summary includes pre-approved fallback clauses from past negotiations (for example, adding a 10-business-day deemed review window to milestone sign-offs), helping commercial leads close terms faster.

## Common Questions

### Can an SME without an in-house legal team use this workflow?

Yes. Most SMEs start by codifying 10 to 15 practical commercial guardrails set by management and finance—such as minimum deposit percentage, maximum payment days, revision caps, and acceptance timelines—to catch common commercial traps early.

### Does AI contract screening replace human legal or financial approval?

No. AI highlights version diffs, numerical mismatches, and deviations from company guardrails; it does not provide legal advice, and authorised managers or external counsel must approve final contract execution.

### What if clients send clean PDFs without tracked changes?

AI compares the new PDF or Word file line by line against your standard template or the previous negotiated draft, surfacing any silent deletions or altered definitions.
